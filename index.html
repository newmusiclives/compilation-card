What Is a TapTunes™ Card?

Physical Media Is Back. This Time It's Smart.

The TapTunes™ card is a format created by TapTunes™: a physical, album-cover-sized collectible card that carries a full release inside it. Tap it with a phone or scan the code and the music plays. No app, no download link in an email, no login. True Fans CONNECT™ has partnered with TapTunes™, who produce every card in this series. It belongs to a new category the industry calls smart media: physical objects with digital content built in. Tap · Listen · Collect.

The object

What's on the card

  • Front: the compilation's artwork, theme and volume — designed like an album cover, produced by TapTunes™ and marked as an official True Fans CONNECT™ release
  • Back: the 16-track listing, your name against your track, an NFC chip and a QR code
  • Inside the chip: a link that opens the full 16-track TapTunes™ player, artwork, liner notes, exclusives and every artist's page
  • It's signable, sellable, postable, and it fits in a wallet, a jacket pocket or a birthday card
The category

Why "smart media"?

Vinyl came back because people want to own something. Streaming stayed because people want convenience. Smart media is the category that does both: a physical product you can hold and give, carrying digital content that never scratches, never runs out, and can be updated after it's sold. Smart vinyl, tap-to-play posters, NFC merch and collectible cards all sit in it. TapTunes™ built the music-card format for that category, and True Fans CONNECT™ partnered with them to bring it to independent artists: TapTunes™ produces the cards, True Fans CONNECT™ curates the compilations and the artists on them. It's the most affordable, most giftable and easiest-to-sell format in the category — which is why it works.

Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

What's included for $500?

One track slot on a 16-track themed compilation, released as an official TapTunes™ collectible card from True Fans CONNECT™, and digitally. We handle release mastering, sequencing, artwork, liner notes, card design and production, the download/stream page, your artist page, and a coordinated launch campaign. You receive 25 cards to sell, The Baseline Audit, and a 90-minute one-to-one call with Paul. You contribute your finished track, a photo, a bio and your links.

Why only 8 slots a month?

Because every slot comes with The Baseline Audit and a 90-minute one-to-one call with Paul, and both are done personally, not by a team. Eight a month is what can be done properly. When a month's eight are gone, the next eight open on the 1st, and you can reserve one in advance.

How does the two-step process work?

Step one: submit your track for review with a $50 slot-holding fee, which reserves your place in this month's eight while we listen. Step two: if your track is accepted, you pay the $450 balance — as one payment or two payments of $275 — and your slot is confirmed. Every submission gets a listen and a decision; you're never charged the balance for a track we haven't accepted.

What if my track isn't accepted?

You're refunded $30 of the $50 and we send you the track review notes. The $20 we keep is the review fee — someone with forty years in the business listened properly to your track, made a decision, and wrote down why. You keep the track and you're welcome to submit a different one, or the same one to a better-fitting theme.

Are payment plans an option?

Yes. Once your track is accepted, the $450 balance can be paid in one payment or two payments of $275. Your slot is confirmed on the first payment either way.

How do the 25 cards work?

Every track owner receives 25 physical cards, shipped to you. Sell them for $20 each — at gigs, online, to your list — and you keep 100% of the money. Twenty-five sales returns your full $500. Want more? Additional cards are available at wholesale.

Who owns my track?

You do. You grant a non-exclusive licence for your track to appear on this compilation, in physical and digital form. You are free to release, stream, sync and sell the same track anywhere else, before, during and after.

Do I earn royalties from the compilation itself?

Your primary return is the 25 cards, which you sell direct and keep 100% of. Terms for any compilation-wide streaming or download income are set out in the participation agreement you receive at checkout.

Is Paul really on the record?

Yes. Track 01 of Vol. 1, Many Roads Traveled, is "I Know Your Name", a song Paul wrote about life on the road. He's not asking you to do anything with your track that he isn't doing with his own — and he's selling his 25 cards the same way you will.

What if my song doesn't fit any of the five themes?

Tell us. New themes are announced as volumes fill, and the best pitch for a sixth compilation may well be yours. If we can't place your track on a theme, we won't take your money for it.

Who makes the cards?

TapTunes™ does. The TapTunes™ card is their format — the card, the NFC chip, the QR code and the player behind it. True Fans CONNECT™ has partnered with TapTunes™ to bring the format to independent artists: TapTunes™ produces every card in the series, and True Fans CONNECT™ curates the compilations, reviews the tracks and runs the launch. Every card carries both marks.

What is The Baseline Audit, exactly?

A 40-question scored diagnostic across craft, audience, live performance and monetisation, taking about 45 minutes. You receive a scored report, a ranked list of what to fix first, and a written 12-month plan you can run yourself. It's sold separately at paulksaunders.com for $500 and is included in full with your track slot.

When is everything due?

Your final master, photo, bio and links are due by November 30, 2026 for Vol. 1. After the sequencing lock, tracks cannot be swapped or re-mastered, even if you submitted early.

What if the compilation doesn't fill?

Each volume goes to print when 16 tracks are confirmed. If a volume hasn't filled by its deadline, you can move to the next open theme or receive a full refund.
